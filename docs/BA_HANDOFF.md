# BA Handoff — Tamil Nadu Women’s DBT Scheme Impact

## Objective
Evaluate beneficiary coverage, payment friction and programme cost across districts, including scenario cost at alternative benefit levels.

## Stakeholders
Programme leadership; district administration; payment operations; grievance/appeals teams; finance; audit/data-governance teams.

## Core requirements
- Compare eligible population with enrolled beneficiaries.
- Track rejection and appeal signals.
- Calculate current and proposed annual cost.
- Identify district-level delivery gaps.
- Protect beneficiary privacy and distinguish illustrative data from official records.

## Decision rule
Prioritise districts with lower coverage and higher payment friction, while using cost scenarios for planning rather than as policy recommendations.

## Acceptance criteria
Coverage and cost KPIs reconcile; district filters work; scenario logic is transparent; synthetic-data disclosure is visible.

## Production controls
Governed beneficiary master, payment-success logs, rejection reasons, appeals, reconciliation, privacy controls, access governance and audit trail.
